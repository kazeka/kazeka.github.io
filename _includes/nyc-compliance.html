What's in CompliCheck NYC

Features available now, in progress and planned.

NYC Building Compliance Check

Enter any New York City address to see its required inspections and filing deadlines (FISP, LL87, LL152, LL84/97, elevators, boilers) and its open violations, cooling towers, fines, hearings and permits, pulled live from NYC Open Data.

Try: Empire State Building · 1 Centre St · 2 MetroTech

Portfolio

All your buildings in one table, ranked by compliance risk. Saved in this browser.

Compliance calendar

Every filing deadline, inspection window, OATH hearing and permit expiry across your portfolio.

Data: NYC DOB, HPD, DOB NOW, OATH, DOHMH, DEP, DCP PLUTO and LL84 datasets via NYC Open Data, plus NYC Planning GeoSearch. Portfolio data stays in your browser (localStorage). Rules are simplified. Check deadlines against official DOB/HPD sources before you act on them.
